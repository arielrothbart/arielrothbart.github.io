Illustrator
Graphic Novel Artist
Storyboard Artist

Focused on narrative-driven imagery,
character design, and sequential storytelling.
Art, and nothing but art.

Illustration, literature, music, films — different mediums, to me, are forms of language.
They are connected ways of expression.

80% of me is made by live theater and music.

You may also know me as "Captain Ariel" from the Rhapsody.
Welcome to "The Rhapsody".
I hope you will have a pleasant journey.
Based in China.

Working across illustration, comics, and visual development,
both independently and in collaboration.

Academy of Art University (MFA)
China University of Geosciences, Wuhan (BFA)
